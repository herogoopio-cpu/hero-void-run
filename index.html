SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE
Use ← / → or A / D to switch between three lanes.

DASH — SPACE
Quickly surge forward and ignore collisions briefly.

SHIELD — Q
Activate a shield that absorbs one hit. Cooldown applies.

MAGNET — E
Attract nearby shards for a few seconds.

TIME SLOW — R
Slow obstacles and the pursuer temporarily.

THE PURSUER
The creature creeps closer over time and surges when you get hit. Collect shards and keep dodging to push it back. If it catches you, the run ends.

PICKUPS
Gold shards increase score and distance. Green cores restore health. Blue orbs refill skill energy.
